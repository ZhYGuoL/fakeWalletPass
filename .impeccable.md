## Design Context

### Users
People who want quick access to events through Apple Wallet by messaging an iMessage agent. They are often moving between events and need a low-friction flow: send a Luma link, get a pass.

### Brand Personality
Premium, cinematic, editorial. The emotional target is confidence and momentum: entry should feel immediate and inevitable.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-first clean cinematic direction with mostly monochrome color. Hero messaging should feel assertive around the "unlock every door" theme. Avoid generic SaaS grids, crypto-neon gradients, and sterile Apple-clone minimalism.

### Design Principles
1. Make the central call-to-action unmistakable and magnetic.
2. Use visible, purposeful motion to explain the iMessage-to-pass flow.
3. Keep visual hierarchy bold: one hero story, one concise explainer section.
4. Preserve accessibility with reduced-motion fallbacks that keep clarity.
5. Keep this site standalone and decoupled from the testing interface.
